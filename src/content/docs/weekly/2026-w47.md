### [SharQ: Bridging Activation Sparsity and FP4 Quantization for LLM Inference](../../papers/arxiv-2606.26587/)

### [AscendGYM: A Cost-Aware Kernel Optimization Benchmark for Real-World Workloads on Ascend NPUs](../../papers/arxiv-2611.17621/)

### [RoCoKV: Accelerating LLM Inference Serving through Uncertainty and Redundancy Aware KV Cache Compression](../../papers/arxiv-2611.25433/)
