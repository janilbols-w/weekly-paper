## 摘要原文

The rapid growth of AI models and the widespread use of distributed training and inference have made inter-accelerator communication a critical system bottleneck. Scale-up fabrics address this bottleneck by combining high per-accelerator bandwidth with low and predictable latency over tightly coupled accelerator domains. However, the appropriate topology, protocol mechanisms, communication semantics, and system integration remain actively debated. We analyze the critical-path communication generated by parallelism strategies and derive the resulting requirements for domain scale, per-accelerator bandwidth, and collective completion latency. We then use these requirements as a framework to examine representative proprietary and open scale-up fabrics, revealing how their physical interconnects, topologies, protocols, and semantics make different trade-offs among performance, scalability, compatibility, and implementation cost. Moreover, based on this analysis and discussions with industry experts, we identify challenges and open problems in physical interconnects and switching, efficient communication, semantics, resource sharing and heterogeneous device interconnection. This paper provides a requirement-driven foundation for analyzing current scale-up fabrics and guiding the design of future systems.
